# Independent literature review and research implications — 24 September 2026

This audit used primary publisher, arXiv, author, and source-code pages. External Lean libraries were not rebuilt; their verification assertions are identified as assertions. The local v6 and existing dated literature note were read. The review found no established proof of Collatz.

## Findings that most change the project's next move

1. **An exact jointly 2-adic/3-adic/real perspective is relevant but already has direct July 2026 prior art.** Oliver Kramer, *Adaptive Search in Collatz Exponent-Code Space via 2-adic and 3-adic Constraints*, submitted **10 July 2026**, [arXiv:2607.10041v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10041v1), calls this the 2–3–∞ diagnostic. It tests real drift, the start residue mod 2^A, and endpoint residue mod 3^L. The elementary necessary-condition theorem says any fixed positive integer's infinite code has vanishing normalized residue-growth rates. Experiments use lengths 100/200/400 and evolutionary search, not exhaustive certificates. No journal publication is listed. **v6's mod 2^(A+1) exact-word condition is stronger than Kramer's necessary mod 2^A start congruence.** Near-critical drift in Kramer is a search choice, not a proved necessity for all divergent orbits; vanishing rates are not a sufficient realizability criterion. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10041v1).

2. **Even the interval/carry formulation has direct code prior art.** [msharpe248/collatz](https://github.com/msharpe248/collatz) advertises `Cylinder.prefix_transport`, `mem_parameters`, `parameters_convex` (prefix endpoints affine in one quotient, finite-height survivor quotients an interval), `WordAffine.adjacent_swap` and `compare_bound` (swap/height/divisibility bounds), and `prefix_power_initial_bound` (repetition measured at the original seed). It reports exclusions of mechanical/Sturmian/automatic itineraries and a complete mechanical-itinerary classification, but explicitly lacks a theorem covering all orbits. It also distinguishes kernel-checked predecessor exponent 0.8676 from informal 0.895 at larger congruence depth. These are source-repository claims, not an independent rebuild or established journal record in this audit. Claim novelty only for a specific additional theorem, integration, or certified infinite family.

3. **A proved coverage theorem can replace an arbitrary A0-only research domain.** Monks, Monks, Monks & Monks, *Strongly sufficient sets and the distribution of arithmetic sequences in the 3x+1 graph*, **Discrete Mathematics 313(4), 468–489, 28 February 2013**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2012.11.019), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1204.3904): §6 defines forward sufficiency as intersecting every divergent shortcut-T orbit, cycle sufficiency as intersecting every nontrivial cycle. Theorem 6.4 proves **20 mod 27 has both**. This does not say every convergent orbit visits it. Applying the theorem to each tail implies recurrent visits on every hypothetical exceptional orbit; it gives no uniform return time. Corollary 5.8 proves every positive T orbit hits 2 mod 9. Table 2 gives the odd classes 3 mod 8 and 5 mod 12 as forward sufficient, hence directly recurrent for divergent odd-only orbits; cycle sufficiency is not supplied by that table. Table 1 gives odd 1 mod 4 as strongly sufficient.

## Exact new local deduction: translate 20 mod 27 to an odd Syracuse section

This is our arithmetic derivation from the preceding coverage result, checked against finite enumeration, **not yet a Lean theorem or a novelty claim**.

Let S(u)=(3u+1)/2^a for u positive odd and a=ν₂(3u+1). The shortcut map T uses (3u+1)/2 at an odd step. The entire episode from u up to S(u) contains the states

    (3u+1)/2^j,   1 ≤ j ≤ a.

A state is 20 modulo 27 iff `3u+1 ≡ 20·2^j (mod 27)`. Since ord_27(2)=18, any such j reduces to a j₀ between 1 and 18 with j₀≤j≤a, so an earlier state of the same episode also hits the section. The congruence forces j₀ odd. After one Syracuse step, 3∤u; eliminating the three j-values forcing 3|u leaves six pairs:

| j₀ | u mod 9 | Additional condition | Equivalent odd arithmetic progression |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | a≥1 (automatic) | u≡13 mod18 |
| 3 | 8 | a≥3 | u≡53 mod72 |
| 7 | 7 | a≥7 | u≡853 mod1152 |
| 9 | 2 | a≥9 | u≡3413 mod4608 |
| 13 | 1 | a≥13 | u≡54613 mod73728 |
| 15 | 5 | a≥15 | u≡218453 mod294912 |

Call the union E. Its six progressions are disjoint (distinct residues mod9). Their natural density is exactly `6935/98304`; the odd-relative density is twice that. An infinite exceptional S orbit visits E infinitely often: every T-tail meets 20mod27, each hit lies in one finite odd episode, and finite many odd episodes cannot contain infinitely many T-hit times. For a nontrivial cycle, E is met on each traversal. No passage-time bound is inferred.

A Python exact-integer check verified the equivalence “the episode hits20mod27 iff u∈E” for every odd u<200000 with3∤u. The proof itself is the modular computation above; the test only detects transcription mistakes.

This yields a concrete continuation: **a Lean-certified first-return system on a section with established universal coverage of exceptional orbits**, carrying exact cylinder and real-height information. It avoids inventing a global coverage hypothesis for an arbitrary family. It does not furnish the difficult descent inequality for the first-return map. First prove the coverage transfer and signed affine transport; then test whether exact interval/rank certificates establish descent or identify irreducible survivor families. Compare with both Kramer and Sharpe before asserting novelty. A finite unresolved frontier or density decay alone remains insufficient.

A particularly important caveat: the six sets are starts of episodes containing a marked T-hit; they are **not** the claim that odd S-values necessarily hit20mod27. Merely taking odd parts of20mod27 would lose the information because powers of2 cycle through all units mod27.

## Established main landmarks and recent primary updates

- **Tao.** *Almost all orbits of the Collatz map attain almost bounded values*, submitted **8 September 2019**, published **Forum of Mathematics, Pi10 (2022), e12**; current arXiv **v7,16 July2026**. For every f(n)→∞, almost every seed in logarithmic density reaches below f(n). This is neither every seed nor convergence to1. Its mechanism uses approximate transport for first-passage variables and a high-frequency estimate for a skew random walk over3-adic cyclic groups. The latest version is a correction/update of the same theorem, not a proof of Collatz. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03562).

- **Inselmann.** *An approximation of the Collatz map and a lower bound for the average total stopping time*, **v1 5February2024; v3 13August2024**. Natural-density-one seeds follow a multiplicative envelope up to the optimal logarithmic horizon `2log(n)/log(4/3)≈6.952log n` for the shortcut map. In particular they reach n^ε for every fixed ε>0. Stronger density than Tao but a different, larger target; neither uniformly dominates the other across all coordinates. No journal reference appears on the current record. Its strengthened density notion and transport are relevant to averaged bridges, but cannot justify pointwise trajectory sampling. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03276v3), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.03276v3).

- **Barina.** The live author project page, generated **24September2026**, states exhaustive convergence verification for starts `<2075·2^60≈2^71.02`. Its log dates `<2^71` to **15January2025**. Published methods/results: *Journal of Supercomputing81,810(2025)*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-025-07337-0). [Live bound](https://pcbarina.fit.vut.cz/). This can supply finite base cases; it supplies neither infinite coverage nor uniform stopping bounds.

- **Angeltveit.** *An improved algorithm for checking the Collatz conjecture for all n<2^N*, **11February2026**, [arXiv:2602.10466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10466). Recursively extends low binary bits, combining descent, mod9-preimage, and path-merging sieves. Its checked fraction tends to zero with scale, and claimed cost for N+1 is less than double N. This is a methodological preprint, not evidence that its projected2^75 or2^77 verification has already occurred. Relevant to a certified adaptive-cylinder engine: reuse arithmetic pruning rather than enumerate every seed. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10466v1).

- **Hercher.** *There are no Collatz-m-Cycles with m≤91*, v3 **4April2023**, *Journal of Integer Sequences26(2023),23.3.5*. Here m counts **local minima**, not odd steps or total length. The same paper explains the finite verification needed for a lower bound of1.375·10^11 odd cycle elements. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.00406v3). Useful comparison for `CycleConstraints`: excluding expanding cycles is elementary sign information; it does not rival these exclusions of contractive candidates.

- **Knight.** *Collatz high cycles do not exist*, *Discrete Mathematics349(3)(March2026),114812*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2025.114812), [publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012365X25004200). High cycles are a specific extremal class of rational cycles, not all cycles. The proof gives a promising small formalizable arithmetic result involving Christoffel words, but external formalizations already exist and must be credited. Note an external README erroneously lists article114425; the publisher gives114812.

- **Rozier–Terracol.** *Paradoxical behavior in Collatz sequences*, **v1 2February2025; v5 17May2026**, published *Discrete Mathematics349(10)(2026),115167*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2026.115167), [current source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00948v5). A paradoxical segment has contracting slope3^q/2^j<1 but T^j(n)≥n. Theorem3.2: an infinite-stopping-time seed produces infinitely many paradoxical segments beginning at powers-of-two multiples of that seed. Hence finiteness of all paradoxical segments would imply Collatz; finiteness remains conjectural. They enumerate593 segments from550 starts, not an exhaustion proof. Strong relation to v6: the positive affine offset must be controlled in addition to slope. v5 corrects LemmaB.2. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.00948v5).

- **Fernández–Ibáñez.** *Christoffel words as extremal structures in Collatz dynamics*, **24July2026**, [arXiv:2607.24844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24844). Proves a max-min extremal statement over cyclic rotations of words with fixed length and weight, identifying Christoffel words. Preprint; useful for designing extremal tests and cycle inequalities, not a universal itinerary theorem. Do not advertise generic balanced-word rigidity as a new project direction without this comparison.

## Unreviewed or freshly formalized 2026 results: separate evidence from status

- **Chang**, *Exploring Collatz Dynamics with Human–LLM Collaboration*, **v1 10March; v6 22April2026**, [arXiv:2603.11066v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11066v6). It reports630 “formal results” and29 paradigms in233pages, explicitly not a Collatz proof. “Formal” in this abstract does not establish a Lean-kernel artifact. Spectral and phantom terminology overlaps the project, but operators require definition-by-definition comparison. The phrase “Paradigm Exhaustion” must not be promoted to a theorem ruling out all future methods. Its listed natural-density/Hausdorff conclusions require their exact ambient sets and hypotheses, not paraphrase as pointwise convergence.

- **Ross**, *Spike Structures and2-adic Transition Laws in the Accelerated Collatz Map*, **Zenodo v3 30August2026**, record modified19September, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22181823). Explicitly states one odd class mod2^(1+sum a) for every finite positive-exponent word. This is direct prior art for the mathematical cylinder theorem; the project's contribution is formal integration, signed precision identities, and subsequent consequences. Finite-word realizability is compatible with lack of positive-integer infinite realization.

- **Mazur/ProofAtlas natural density**, manuscript **v2 21July2026**, [page](https://www.proofatlas.ai/formalizations/natural-density-log-time-collatz/). Claims every diverging threshold is reached by natural-density-one starts within145log N odd steps or436log N raw steps. The site's “accepted formalization” is an internal process; its listed reviewer is an AI and independence is explicitly not asserted. The page gives source and evidence but no independent external priority timestamp. Potentially important if verified; not an established journal theorem in this review.

- **Mazur positive density**, **manuscript v2.1 6September2026**, [page](https://www.proofatlas.ai/formalizations/positive-density-log-time-collatz/). Claims a fixed explicit positive fraction reaches1 within10.46log n raw steps. The page states review pending, reuse of compiled dependencies, no independent LeanChecker run, no complete paper-wide review. **Positive lower density of predecessors**, companion **6September2026**, [page](https://www.proofatlas.ai/formalizations/positive-lower-density-collatz-predecessors/), separately claims positive lower density for predecessors of each fixed target3∤a, implying conditionally that upper-density-one convergence would force universal convergence. The premise is not proved. This would be structurally significant if independently confirmed and should be audited rather than automatically imported.

- **Shaik**, *Polylogarithmic Descent for Almost All Collatz Orbits in Natural Density*, current **v3.2.4**, [author page](https://shaikidris.github.io/), [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22130385). Claims polylog targets, near-optimal logarithmic shortcut time, quantitative exceptional rates, and height control through the witness. The author links a Palomar v2 verification of four named declarations; this distinguishes it from unsupported prose but is not the same as a journal review of every claim. Its target remains growing, not1. The important proposed mechanism is exact source-fiber transport at first crossing with a compressed time set. No independent full code replay performed here.

## Classical priority essential to honest novelty claims

Terras, **Acta Arithmetica30(1976),241–252**, [publisher](https://www.impan.pl/en/publishing-house/journals-and-series/acta-arithmetica/all/30/3/101028/a-stopping-time-problem-on-the-positive-integers), supplies the parity-vector/cylinder and stopping-time foundations. Bernstein–Lagarias, **Canadian Journal of Mathematics48(6)(1996),1154–1169**, [author PDF](https://websites.umich.edu/~lagarias/doc/bernstein.pdf), gives the2-adic conjugacy for the **shortcut map T**, a continuous map on all Z₂. This does not assert a global continuous accelerated-Syracuse map at−1/3; v6's correction properly separates the models. The finite cylinder result can be recovered from parity coding by including the final odd bit, so Ross is not its oldest conceptual ancestry.

## Recommended research positioning

The most defensible distinctive next package is: **“Certified return sections and arithmetic survivor frontiers for accelerated Collatz dynamics.”** Combine the above six-port section (a proved coverage deduction), exact exponent cylinders, signed affine height constraints, and counterexample-guided finite-certificate generation. The output should be an independently checkable theorem on an explicit nontrivial infinite family or a theorem quantifying why a finite abstract ranking fails. A pointwise certificate for all return branches would solve the difficult problem and must remain a clearly marked open target. No amount of smaller averaged spectral radius alone supplies it.
