# Research and release conventions

- Keep the repositories public. Do not change repository visibility.
- Work locally and commit useful checkpoints as needed. Do not push incremental
  research automatically. Push only when an explicitly agreed research or release
  milestone has been completed, or when the user expressly requests a push.
- The authorized v7 milestone includes integrating all previous work into `main`,
  preparing and verifying the English paper and source archive, updating the
  standalone CollatzShadowing snapshot where appropriate, and publishing the new
  version on Zenodo. It does not authorize unrelated announcements or messages.
- Preserve all published PDFs, their hashes, and their original supplementary
  snapshots. Versions v1-v7 are frozen; v7 is published at
  `10.5281/zenodo.24284425`. Further paper versions belong to the existing
  Zenodo record series, with concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.20021537`.
- Distinguish checked Lean declarations, paper proofs, finite executable checks,
  external results, and open assumptions. Cite precedents and make no unsupported
  claim of mathematical priority or of solving the Collatz conjecture.
- New publication materials, release metadata, and release documentation are in
  English. Historical research notes retain their original language and content.
